Usage information

Last updated: September 8, 2026. This page describes the project's operating boundaries; it is not a substitute for installation-specific agreements or legal advice.

Personal access

This is a private self-hosted tool with no public registration. Connect only an account you are authorized to use. Access to shared calendars remains subject to the permissions granted by their owners.

Read-only Google boundary

The initial Google integration reads information. It does not send email or edit Google Calendar. Derived dashboard entries and reminders are local application records; they do not alter their original Google sources.

Check important information

Source data, automated interpretation, and notifications can be incomplete, delayed, or incorrect. Review linked original information and resolve uncertain items before relying on a date or action. A reminder being dismissed is not confirmation that its underlying task was completed.

Availability and control

Availability depends on the self-hosted installation, connected devices, network access, and third-party services. This project does not promise uninterrupted delivery. The account owner can withdraw Google access and ask the operator to remove locally retained information.

Questions

Contact the operator who supplied access to your installation. See privacy information for data use and retention details.
