Privacy information

Last updated: September 8, 2026.

Purpose and access

Personal Command Center is a self-hosted personal tool. Its Google connection requests Gmail read-only, Calendar events read-only, and Calendar list read-only access. Calendar access includes calendars that the connected account is allowed to read. Application filtering initially targets school and family scheduling messages.

The current connection proof checks account identity, lists matching message identifiers, and reads calendar identifiers and bounded event identifiers. It does not retrieve message bodies for that proof. Future scheduling workflows will need selected message contents and event details to identify dates and actions.

Storage and protection

The installation stores application records on its operator-controlled server. Google refresh credentials are stored privately outside source control and outside the public site. Access tokens and credentials must not appear in dashboard responses or ordinary job logs. These public pages contain no account data and provide no connection to the private database.

Cloud processing

Where configured and explicitly disclosed to the account owner, a cloud AI service may receive selected in-scope email content to interpret scheduling information. A cloud interpreter has not been represented here as active or selected. Before real-message interpretation is enabled, the installation must identify the service, content sent, purpose, applicable provider retention, and processing limits. Local storage of results does not mean a cloud provider retains no data.

Use and sharing

Google data is used to provide the requested personal dashboard and scheduling functions. It is not sold, used for advertising, or supplied to data brokers. This project does not use Google data to train its own general-purpose AI models. Any future service configuration must preserve these stated boundaries and disclose relevant processing before it is enabled.

Retention and deletion

A fixed automatic retention and deletion schedule has not yet been implemented. Local records may remain until the operator deletes them. Revoking Google access stops future authorized fetching but does not automatically erase previously stored records, backups, or data subject to a provider's own retention rules. Contact the installation operator to remove local data and credentials and to clarify backup retention.

You can review or remove this application's Google access through your Google Account connections.

Contact and changes

Contact the operator who supplied access to your installation for questions or deletion requests. If a workflow adds a new use or recipient of Google data, its disclosure and necessary authorization must be updated before that processing starts.
