Personal Command Center

A personal, self-hosted dashboard for reviewing schedules, reminders, and selected information in one place.

Google connection

The connection requests read-only access to Gmail messages, Google Calendar events, and the list of calendars available to the connected account. It does not request permission to send email, delete messages, mark mail read, or change Google Calendar events.

The first planned email workflow focuses on school and family scheduling. Gmail permission covers the mailbox; workflow filtering is an application rule, not a narrower Google permission.

Current availability

This is a private personal project, not a public sign-up service. Read-only connection setup has been tested. Automated refresh, interpretation, and notification workflows are being developed and are not represented here as fully operational.

These pages provide public information only. The dashboard and its account data are hosted separately and are not exposed by this site.

Read the privacy information and usage information before connecting an account. For questions, contact the operator who supplied access to your installation.
